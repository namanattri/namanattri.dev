I'm Naman, a backend engineer inspired by my passion for problem-solving and system design. I enjoy working with JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Node.js, Golang, blockchain and Ethereum, distributed systems, Docker, Kubernetes, Infrastructure as Code with Terraform and cloud computing, and more recently with DevOps and Gen & Agentic AI.

Over my career I've tackled challenging problems and designed complex systems, and I keep sharpening my skills by reading books and tech blogs and talking with my peers. I value the fresh perspectives that junior engineers bring, and I like to help them grow their soft skills alongside their technical ones.

What motivates me is delivering features, working well with my team and solving problems together. I balance my personal and professional goals with the support of my wife, daughter, mother, father, sister and brother-in-law, whom I love dearly and feel blessed to have in my life. Their belief, motivation and support have been instrumental in my achievements so far. I cherish the time I spend with my family, especially on outings to leisure places and religious sites.

I believe in sharing knowledge so others can benefit from it, which is why I created this blog to collect what I've learned and achieved so far. I'm a lifelong learner and a curator of knowledge, because once a student, always a student. I'm not an expert and still have a lot to learn, so I welcome feedback and suggestions, both positive and corrective.
